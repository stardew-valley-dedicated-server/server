FarmhouseStack exit-warp E2E flake root-caused and fixed (was the review cycle's open investigation): vanilla clients re-derive interior exit warps locally from the building position while deserializing a location introduction (`buildings.OnValueAdded` → `Building.updateInteriorWarps`, unguarded), so the intro-time rewrite is always clobbered client-side and only a post-introduction delta restores it; a `NetInt` equal-value write emits no delta (so a rejoin whose targets were already correct got no delta — broken exit all session, a real production bug); and a delta's value stays invisible until the interpolation window elapses (`InterpolationWait`, 15 client ticks — 3s at `CLIENT_TPS=5`).
